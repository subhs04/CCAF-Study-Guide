# Domain Weights & Exam Format

← [Quick Reference index](./README.md)

---

## Exam format

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Total questions | 60 |
| Format | Multiple choice (4 options, 1 correct) |
| Scenarios | 4 scenarios (drawn randomly from 6 available) |
| Passing score | 720 / 1000 |
| Unanswered | Scored as incorrect — always guess |
| Time | ~90 minutes recommended |

---

## How scoring works

- Scaled score 100–1000 (not a direct percentage)
- Raw correct answers are weighted across domains
- Minimum passing: 720
- Your practice: 545 (31/60 correct, 9 unanswered)
- Gap to pass: ~175 scaled points ≈ roughly 10–12 additional correct answers
